About

We work at the threshold.

The moment a guest arrives is the moment everything either clicks — or doesn't. It's the first impression, the emotional tone, the signal that tells them whether they made the right choice coming here.

Studio Drempel was founded by Parker, an American hospitality and guest experience consultant with over a decade of large-scale operations experience at some of the most-visited attractions in the United States — including Busch Gardens and Hershey Entertainment.

We are headquartered in the United States and expanding into the European market with an established presence in the Netherlands. That dual footing gives our clients something rare: deep American attractions expertise with genuine boots-on-the-ground access to the European hospitality landscape.

Whether you're a US operator looking to understand international guests, or a European attraction ready to raise your standard — we work across both worlds by design.

Services

Guest Experience Strategy

End-to-end auditing and redesign of the guest journey — from first contact through departure. We identify where friction lives and replace it with moments guests remember. Available remotely or on-site across the US and Europe.

02

Operations Consulting

High-volume park and attraction operations are a discipline. We bring proven systems for crowd flow, staff deployment, parking, and capacity management — refined at scale inside American attractions and applied to your context.

Transatlantic Readiness

US operators expanding to Europe, or European operators looking to better serve American visitors — we sit at that intersection by design. We translate expectations across cultures and turn them into concrete operational changes.

Experience built at scale.

Most hospitality consultants have worked in boutique environments. Parker's background is different — built inside some of the highest-volume guest operations in North America, where getting the arrival moment right isn't a philosophy, it's an operational necessity.

That foundation, combined with an active European presence and deep familiarity with the Dutch and broader Benelux hospitality market, gives Studio Drempel a perspective that very few consultants on either continent can offer.

We are American in expertise, transatlantic in reach.
